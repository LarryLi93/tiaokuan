GetLifes uses the information you enter to calculate an educational estimate of remaining lifespan. Your profile details are used only to provide this experience.

Information we use

This may include your birth date, sex, country, height, weight, and lifestyle answers. We do not use this information to diagnose, treat, or prevent any medical condition.

Data storage

All of your profile data is stored locally on your device only (via the system's local storage). We do not upload any data to a server or any third party. We cannot see or access your personal information.

Data retention

Your profile data stays on your device until you clear it from the in-app Settings. Because we never receive your data, we keep no copy of it.

Children

GetLifes is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), do not enter personal information, or have a parent or guardian do so on your behalf.

Your choices

You can review or change your profile information at any time, and clear all data with one tap from the in-app Settings. Do not enter information that you do not want used in the calculation.

Health disclaimer

The lifespan estimate provided by this app is based on public statistics and is for reference only. It is not medical advice or a diagnosis.
